# Learn

Guided reading, two tiers deep:

- **[Guides](/learn/guides/)** — a beginner-level walk through the kinds of objects enumeratio counts, ranks, and
  draws, live in the browser, no combinatorics background assumed.
- **[Explorations](/learn/explorations/)** — deep-dive essays for a reader who already has the vocabulary the
  guides build, going further into a handful of structures.
