- Named by a generator because past degree 2 orders aren't determined by their discriminant; its index in [[AlgebraicIntegers]] is $\sqrt{\mathrm{disc}\,\mathbb{Z}[a] / d_K}$
- A value for `Over`, as [[AlgebraicIntegers]] is; at a prime dividing the index, $\mathcal{O}/(x)$ being a field is still what makes $(x)$ prime
