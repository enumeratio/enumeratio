# aestimatio

_aestimatio_: a valuation, an estimate. The mathematical core — what the heads **are**: the
numbers, functions and algebras, defined on the [kernel](/nucleus/) and evaluated there.
Both senses of the word are meant.

- **Valuation.** Arithmetic that knows where a number lives: residues and `IntegerMod`, integer
  valuations, profinite integers and the [adèles](/guide/adeles/),
  [numeral systems](/guide/numerals/) from factoradic to Zeckendorf, Gaussian integers and
  Hermite normal form — all over bigints, all exact.
- **Estimation.** Special functions evaluated honestly: the Hurwitz and two-argument zeta,
  the [Lerch transcendent](/explore/lerchphi/), the [polylogarithm](/explore/polylog/),
  Barnes G, with `N(x, d)` correctly rounded to the digits asked for — a number with a
  stated error, not a float that happens to print well.

## What it defines

Every head is documented in the [**symbol reference**](/reference/symbol/), with examples
that are also its tests, and cross-checked against other systems wherever a claim can be
computed.

- **Arithmetic** — residues, number theory, numerals, adèles and idèles.
- **Analysis** — the special functions above, to arbitrary precision.
- **Algebras** — [hypercomplex](/guide/hypercomplex/) units (multicomplex, split, dual,
  Clifford, Grassmann) and the geometric algebra over them;
  [diagram](/guide/diagram-algebras/), [Hecke](/guide/hecke/),
  [incidence](/guide/incidence/), [path](/guide/quiver/), [Hopf](/guide/hopf/) and
  [group](/guide/groupalgebra/) algebras, several on one engine through one shared seam.
- **Groups** — [braid groups](/guide/braid/) and their knots, and
  [the modular group](/guide/modular/).

The [guides](/guide/) follow each family far enough to see how it is built; the
[explorations](/explore/) put its constants on dials.
