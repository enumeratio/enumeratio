# Complex Bases

Numbers written in a base $\beta$ from the Gaussian or Eisenstein integers, every numeral of at
most $L$ places one tile. Drag the base $\beta$ or a digit; add and remove digits; switch the
lattice; take the base off the lattice altogether; or roll a random system. The caption names
the system when it is a notable one or a favorite, and its menu jumps to any of them. The
[guide](/docs/complex-numerals/complex-bases) explains why one digit per residue class makes
every numeral a different point.
