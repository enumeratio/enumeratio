enumeratio

Latin ēnumerātiō, from ēnumerāre, “to count out”: ē-, “out”, and numerāre, “to count”, from numerus, “number”. Numeration is writing a number down; enumeration is counting and listing the things numbers count. Both come from the same root, and this site is about both.

enumeratio is a home for mathematical objects, each at an address meant to last: the kinds of numbers and the ways of writing them, integer sequences, permutations, partitions, paths, trees and the other structures of combinatorics, the statistics on them and the maps between them, the polytopes whose faces they label, knots.

One object, one page, one address.

The site is being rebuilt. To compute with these objects today, use notatio.
